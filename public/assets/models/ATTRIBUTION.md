# Astronaut model attribution

`acca-astronaut.glb` is an optimized derivative of `Astronaut.glb` from the
Google `<model-viewer>` shared assets collection.

- Creator: Poly
- Source: https://github.com/google/model-viewer/tree/master/packages/shared-assets/models
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 (CC BY 2.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/

Changes made for ACCA EDU: texture downscaling and WebP recompression, glTF
packing optimization, runtime material treatment, pose deformation, and
additional modular mission equipment.
